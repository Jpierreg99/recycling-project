Project Context

Bogotá faces major environmental and waste management challenges due to rapid urban growth, increasing population density, and inefficient recycling infrastructure distribution.

The Smart Recycling Recommendation System was designed to support sustainability strategies using Machine Learning techniques and urban data analysis.

Expected Impact

The Smart Recycling Recommendation System aims to improve urban sustainability strategies by supporting data-driven decision-making processes related to recycling infrastructure distribution.

The project may contribute to better waste management, increased recycling participation, and optimization of environmental policies in Bogotá.

Key Project Objectives

Improve Recycling

Identify areas with low recycling coverage and support optimized waste collection.

Data Analytics

Analyze demographic and environmental patterns using Machine Learning models.

Urban Sustainability

Support environmental decision-making processes through data-driven insights.
