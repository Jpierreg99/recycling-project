Introduction

CRISP-ML is a structured methodology used to develop Machine Learning projects through organized phases, ensuring analytical quality, business understanding, and scalable data solutions.

Methodology Phases

Business Understanding

Identification of sustainability challenges, recycling inefficiencies, and project goals.

Data Understanding

Exploration of demographic, waste generation, and recycling datasets from Bogotá.

Data Engineering

Cleaning, preprocessing, feature engineering, normalization, and clustering preparation.

Machine Learning

Application of K-Means clustering algorithms to identify urban sustainability patterns.

Why CRISP-ML?

The CRISP-ML methodology provides a professional structure for Machine Learning projects, improving reproducibility, analytical quality, and project organization.
